# Sales Performance Analysis  

A portfolio project analyzing sales performance using transactional data. The project focuses on exploring KPI metrics, sales trends, product performance, regional contribution, and delivering actionable business recommendations.  

**Tools:** SQL, BigQuery, Looker Studio, PowerPoint  
**Dataset:** Simulated transactional dataset  

---

## Overview  
As businesses grow, understanding sales performance becomes critical for strategic decision-making. This project analyzes transaction data to evaluate overall performance, identify high-performing products and categories, assess regional contributions, and present insights through an interactive dashboard and structured presentation.  

---

## Key Metrics  
- **Financial:** Total Revenue, Average Order Value (AOV)  
- **Sales:** Total Orders, Monthly Sales Trend (2020–2021)  
- **Product:** Sales & Quantity by Category, Top 5 Products  
- **Regional:** Sales & Quantity by City  

---

## Business Questions  
- How did the business perform overall?  
- How did sales trends evolve over time?  
- Which product categories contributed most to revenue and quantity?  
- Which cities generated the highest sales and orders?  
- What recommendations can support future growth?  

---

## Methodology  
1. Define business objectives  
2. Explore relational database structure  
3. Write SQL queries in BigQuery to extract insights  
4. Visualize results using Looker Studio  
5. Build a presentation with key findings and recommendations  

---

## Key Findings  
- High revenue with strong AOV indicates premium product dominance  
- Sales peaked in May 2021 but dropped sharply in September 2021  
- Robots generated the highest revenue, while eBooks dominated quantity  
- Washington emerged as the strongest market in both sales and quantity  

---

## Recommendations  
- Maintain premium product momentum with bundling and loyalty programs  
- Increase order volume through targeted campaigns  
- Stabilize post-peak sales with regular promotions and diversified channels  
- Optimize mid-tier categories (Robot Kits, Drone Kits) for balanced growth
