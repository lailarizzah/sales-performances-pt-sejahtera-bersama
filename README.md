# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard  
Analyzing sales trends, product performance, and market contribution to drive business insights and recommendations.

---

## Project Background & Overview  
This dataset represents transactional sales records across multiple product categories and cities. The objective of this project is to evaluate overall business performance, identify high-value and high-volume products, assess regional market strength, and provide actionable recommendations for sustaining growth.  

The analysis covers sales transactions between January 2020 and September 2021, focusing on both financial and operational metrics. This report is compiled as part of a portfolio project to demonstrate end-to-end data analysis, visualization, and storytelling skills.  

---

## Northstar Metrics  
- **Revenue & AOV**: Total revenue generated and average order value.  
- **Sales Trend**: Monthly revenue fluctuations and seasonal peaks.  
- **Product Performance**: Sales vs. quantity by category, Top 5 products.  
- **Regional Contribution**: Total sales and quantity by city.  

---

## Technical Implementation & Architecture  

**Tech Stack & Tools:**  
- SQL / BigQuery (data extraction & aggregation)  
- Looker Studio (interactive dashboard visualization)  
- Excel / Google Sheets (data validation & quick checks)  
- PowerPoint (business presentation & storytelling)  

**Dataset Structure:**  
- Relational tables: Customers, Orders, Products, ProductCategory  
- Key columns include: `OrderID`, `CustomerID`, `ProductID`, `Category`, `Revenue`, `Quantity`, `City`, `OrderDate`

## Executive Summary & Insights  

**Overall Performance**  
- Strong revenue driven by premium products with high AOV.  

**Sales Trend**  
- Peak in May 2021, sharp decline in September 2021 → seasonal demand patterns.  

**Product Performance**  
- Robots dominate revenue, while eBooks dominate quantity.  
- Mid-tier categories (Robot Kits, Drone Kits) show balanced potential.  

**Regional Contribution**  
- Washington is the strongest market in both sales and quantity.  
- Other cities contribute moderately, with premium-driven sales in Houston and Sacramento.  

---

## Recommendations  
- **Premium Products**: Maintain momentum with bundling and loyalty programs.  
- **Order Growth**: Launch campaigns to increase order frequency (e.g., “buy more, save more”).  
- **Trend Stabilization**: Smooth post-peak fluctuations with regular promotions and diversified channels.  
- **Category Optimization**: Focus on mid-tier categories (Robot Kits, Drone Kits) for balanced growth.
